Everything your miners report, in one window

Find them, watch them, tune them. MinerGuru talks to AxeOS and NerdOS directly over your local network.

  • Finds your miners

    Scans your local network in seconds, or add a miner by IP address or hostname.

  • Live fleet dashboard

    Total hashrate with its history, power, efficiency in J/TH, shares, best difficulty and pool latency.

  • In the menu bar

    Your fleet hashrate always in sight, with quick access to every miner.

  • Tune and control

    Frequency, core voltage and fan for Eco, Standard and Boost. Restart or rename in a click. Overclocking is always at your own risk.

  • Pool management

    A pool library, assignment to every slot of every miner and a latency test before you switch.

  • Solo mining odds

    Your chance of finding a block per day, month or year, for BTC and BCH.

  • Private by design

    No account, no cloud, no tracking. MinerGuru talks to your miners on your own network, and to a pool or mail server only for features you use. Once a day it asks GitHub for the latest version, which you can turn off. Hide addresses for screenshots with one shortcut.

  • Alerts

    A notification when a miner goes offline, overheats, stops finding shares or falls back to its backup pool, and when a payout address changes. Also by email through your own mail server.

  • Long-term history

    Hashrate, temperatures, efficiency, power and error rate stored locally in SQLite: every minute for two weeks, hourly for three years. Export it as CSV.

  • Scheduled pool switching

    Move miners to a saved pool at a set time on set weekdays, for example to a solo pool on nights and weekends, and back again.

  • Maintenance

    Plan firmware checks, fan cleaning, dusting, thermal paste and more, get a reminder when they are due, and keep a record of what was done.

  • Reports

    Uptime, output, energy and cost per miner for a day, a week or a month, with the maintenance done in that time. Save them as PDF or CSV.

  • Automatic restart

    Optional: restart a miner that stops finding shares, or one stuck on its backup pool after the primary is back. At most once an hour.

  • Read-only mode and change log

    Lock the app so it cannot change anything on a miner, and look up every change in a local log. Protected NerdOS miners work with their one-time codes.

The same app on your Windows PC

MinerGuru for Windows looks and works like the Mac app: the same dashboard, pools and power tools, built natively with WinUI. It sits in the notification area and keeps watching while the window is closed.

Your fleet in your pocket

The Android app has the same dashboard, miner details, pool tools, maintenance and reports, built natively in Material You. It even keeps an eye on your miners while it is closed.

  • Material You

    Takes its colors from your wallpaper, follows dark mode and runs edge to edge.

  • Alerts in the background

    Battery-friendly checks every 15 minutes, or every minute when you need to know right away.

  • Live log

    The miner's own log streamed live, with filter, search and sharing.

  • Pools and tuning

    The same pool library, slot assignment and Eco, Standard and Boost profiles as on the Mac.

Widgets for every screen

Hashrate, health and your solo odds without opening the app: on the Mac desktop, on your Android home screen and on the Windows widget board.

Mac

Desktop and Notification Center

  • FleetTotal hashrate, status and power of all miners, in three sizes.
  • MinerOne miner with hashrate and temperature. You pick which.
  • Solo oddsHow likely your fleet is to find a block, for each coin.
  • PowerPower, energy and cost of the fleet today.

They keep updating while MinerGuru is closed. On macOS 26, Control Center gets controls for the fleet, for night mode (every miner at Eco with a quiet fan) and for muting alerts until morning.

Android

Home screen · Material You

  • FleetFour sizes. Tall enough, it lists every miner.
  • MinerOne miner, chosen when you place the widget.
  • Solo oddsYour chance of finding a block, for each coin.

Colored by your wallpaper and kept fresh by the background checks, even while the app is closed.

Windows

Widget board · Windows 11

  • FleetTotal hashrate, status and power of all miners, in three sizes.
  • Solo oddsYour chance of finding a block, small or medium.

Needs Developer Mode. MinerGuru is not signed, and Windows only accepts widgets from unsigned apps in Developer Mode. Turn it on under Settings › System › For developers, then switch the widgets on in MinerGuru's settings. Without it, MinerGuru works as usual from the notification area.

Download

Straight from the developer, without an app store or an account. All apps are free.

Mac

macOS 14 or later · Apple Silicon

  1. Download MinerGuru.dmg and open it.
  2. Drag MinerGuru into Applications and open it from there.
  3. MinerGuru is not notarized by Apple, so macOS asks you to confirm the first start. Click Done, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway.
Download for MacMinerGuru.dmg

Windows

Windows 11 · x64 and Arm

  1. Download MinerGuru-x64.exe, or MinerGuru-arm64.exe for an Arm PC.
  2. The installer is not signed, so SmartScreen asks you to confirm. Click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Open MinerGuru from the Start menu and let it scan your network for miners.
Download for WindowsWindows 11

Smart App Control blocks unsigned apps and has no exception for a single app. If it is on, turn it off under Windows Security → App & browser control to use MinerGuru.

Android

  1. Download MinerGuru.apk on your phone and open it.
  2. When Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps.
  3. Open MinerGuru and allow access to devices on your local network, so it can find your miners.
Download for AndroidMinerGuru.apk

MinerGuru is published anonymously, so Google has not verified its developer. Where Google enforces this, Android refuses the install until you allow it once: in Developer options turn on Apps from unverified developers, restart and wait 24 hours. That applies in Brazil, Indonesia, Singapore and Thailand since September 2026, and Google plans to extend it worldwide. Installing with adb works without this step.

Want updates automatically? Add github.com/GuruMeditationSoftware/minerguru-android in Obtainium.
